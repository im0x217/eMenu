# Visual Direction, Textures, Iconography, and Anti-AI-Tells

Art-direction standards for imagery, tactile surfaces, custom iconography, palette control, and the 9-axis Style Variation Engine.

## Creative Image Direction

Treat imagery as primary layout architecture, not filler thumbnails:
- **Preferred treatments:** photography-led onboarding, large editorial image blocks, image-backed headers, product/lifestyle photography, scenic/atmospheric backgrounds, illustration-driven entry screens, layered media cards, bold visual covers, media shelves/carousels, and backgrounds partially revealed behind typography.
- Push hero imagery and visual storytelling harder whenever the app category supports it while ensuring different screens remain part of one product world.

## Background Texture & Surface Rules

Never default to sterile flat white or flat dark fills. Introduce controlled tactile atmosphere where appropriate:
- **Allowed treatments:** soft film grain, subtle noise, matte paper texture, lightly speckled surfaces, brushed/frosted feel, tonal gradient fog, clouded ambient depth, tactile matte surfaces, faint technical grid/pattern, or blurred photographic depth layers.
- **Guardrail:** texture must support mood and premium tactility without competing with typography or reducing legibility.

## Image-Behind-Text Treatments

When placing imagery behind or beneath typography:
- Use bottom-to-top gradient fades, side fade masks (leaving clean resting zones for copy), soft blur overlays, image bleeding behind copy into background tone, or edge-to-edge visuals with a clean scrim beneath headline and CTA.
- Useful for onboarding, welcome screens, media, travel, fashion, lifestyle, premium commerce, social, and editorial apps.
- Never place text over raw busy imagery, muddy overlays, or stacked heavy gradients that break hierarchy.

## Creative Assets & Custom Iconography

### Restrained Supporting Assets
Use tasteful, system-integrated accents when they elevate identity: clean micro-illustrations, simple geometric vector motifs, subtle line-art accents, dotted guides, arc shapes, orbital lines, understated starbursts, calm abstract marks, mini diagram elements, product-relevant iconography, or clean sticker-like accents where brand-appropriate. Avoid sticker spam, childish doodles (unless brand-driven), or meaningless vector noise.

### Iconography Rule
Reject generic open-source/developer-tool icon vibes (including bland default Lucide-like template sets) and inconsistent mixed weights/strokes. Aim for a cohesive, custom-feeling, product-specific icon system with consistent stroke or fill weight.

## Mobile Anti-AI-Tells

Strictly avoid unless explicitly requested:
- **Visual tells:** purple-blue fintech startup gradients, arbitrary glass cards, purposeless ambient blobs, fake neon "premium" glow, dribbble floating widget clones, oversized corner radii on everything, over-rendered glossy surfaces without hierarchy.
- **Layout tells:** fake chart dashboard spam, repeated meaningless stat cards, 12-widget homepages fighting for focus, cloned flow screens, empty oversized cards, phone-shaped websites.
- **Copy tells:** filler slogans (*elevate your life*, *unlock your potential*, *next-gen finance*, *seamless control*, *smarter than ever*, *transform your day*) and fake brand names (*Acme*, *NovaCore*, *Flowbit*, *Quantix*, *VeloPay*).
- **UI clutter tells:** excess pills, badges, tiny status labels, fake OS markers, meaningless avatar rows, random chart inserts, decorative toggles with no product purpose.

## Color, Non-Genericity, and Media Framing

### Color Palette Discipline
Let 1–2 accents do real work over clean base surfaces. Avoid muddy accidental mixes, default purple-blue gradients, or unrestrained rainbow saturation. Whether bold, dark, soft, editorial, or playful, keep internal color logic tight and brand-specific.

### Non-Genericity & "Not Always Simple, Always Clean"
Do not settle for clone templates or force hyper-minimal emptiness. Rich layering, tactile depth, multiple visual zones, and expressive art direction are encouraged whenever hierarchy stays crisp and readable. Rule: **not always simple — always clean**.

### Image System & Fixed Media Frames
When an app category benefits from photography/illustration (social, commerce, travel, wellness, editorial, food, fashion, creator, marketplace), integrate real image storytelling inside **fixed, repeatable media frames** (consistent aspect ratios, predictable crop rules, coherent corner radii — no random image proportions or lazy thumbnails).
